About Sigma Xi

Founded in 1886, Sigma Xi is one of the oldest and largest scientific honor societies, with more than 500 chapters globally. Membership is granted by nomination and election to individuals who have demonstrated noteworthy research achievement. Notable members include Albert Einstein, Linus Pauling, and Enrico Fermi.
