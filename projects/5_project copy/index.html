My Raptors Fellow Certificate
Proud to be recognized as a Fellow Raptor - part of a distinguished community of over 550 innovators shaping breakthrough solutions with Raptors.dev.

As a Raptors Fellow, I am part of an exclusive, peer-selected community within the Hackathon Raptors Association , dedicated to shaping the future of software through impactful innovation, mentorship, and cross-sector collaboration.

This prestigious fellowship reflects my contributions to:

  • Leading enterprise-scale system architecture and transformation—including architecting Walmart’s Fulfillment Management System (FMS), which processes millions of messages daily and improved operational efficiency.
  • Advancing the field through published research in distributed systems, digital risk management, and cloud-native applications.
  • Driving DevOps automation and microservices adoption to enable scalable, resilient architectures.
  • Supporting global tech education as a judge and mentor at premier hackathons including MIT, Princeton, Berkeley, and Harvard.
  • Empowering the next generation of innovators through my role as a Top 1% Mentor on ADPList, with 16,000+ global peers.
